# Cholk demo

The demo shows Cholk's canvas, a real Twitch chat suggestion, streamer approval, and the approved idea displayed through OBS on a Twitch broadcast. It also shows the Codex development conversation and outlines possible next steps.

## Reproduce the flow

1. Start Cholk and its Twitch relay using [TWITCH_SETUP.md](TWITCH_SETUP.md).
2. In Twitch chat, send `!branch Floating city | A city above the clouds`.
3. Open **Submission queue** and approve the suggestion.
4. Show the approved idea in the OBS Browser Source and on the Twitch broadcast.
5. Show how Codex was used during development.

The recorded demo uses real footage. Freehand drawing, AI-assisted diagram conversion, and AI agents are presented as future ideas. The separate AI conversion experiment is not integrated into the main app.

Before sharing a recording, check that no tokens, secrets, or unapproved private information are visible.
