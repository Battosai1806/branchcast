# Cholk — submission notes

Cholk is a shared canvas for Twitch streamers and their communities. Viewers can submit a local canvas remix or suggest an idea through Twitch chat. The streamer reviews each submission and chooses what to feature in OBS.

The team used OpenAI Codex to build the application, Twitch relay, review flow, OBS display, and tests. A real Twitch chat message was approved and shown through OBS on a live Twitch broadcast on November 2, 2026.
